## Restoration emergencies common in Meiners Oaks

Meiners Oaks sits in the Ojai Valley, tucked against the foothills with the Ventura River corridor running nearby, and that geography drives most of the calls we get. Winter storms push runoff down from the surrounding hillsides faster than older drainage can carry it, which means crawlspaces and low-lying slabs take on water even when the rain itself wasn't extreme. Summer and fall bring the opposite problem: dry brush, low humidity, and Santa Ana wind events that put the valley's wildland-urban interface homes at real risk for smoke infiltration, even on properties that never saw direct flame. Add in a housing stock where plumbing hasn't always kept pace with the decades, and burst pipes and slow leaks behind plaster walls are just as common as weather-driven losses.
