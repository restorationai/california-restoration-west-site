## What Should You Actually Check Before Hiring a Mold Remediation Company?

The biggest factors are IICRC certification, a written containment plan, and post-remediation verification, not just a star rating. A high review count tells you a company has done volume; it does not tell you whether the last job used proper containment or ran a clearance test when finished.

Ask any company you're considering three things: do they follow the [IICRC S520 standard](https://iicrc.org/s520/) for containment and negative air pressure, do they test and photograph moisture levels before and after the work, and do they provide a written scope before starting. A company that can't answer those clearly is a company you should keep calling around on.

Also ask how they handle the source of the moisture. Mold remediation that doesn't address the underlying leak, whether it's a roof intrusion, a slow plumbing leak, or hydrostatic pressure in a crawl space, is a temporary fix. If you're not sure what's causing the moisture in the first place, [our guide on how to test for mold](/blog/how-to-test-for-mold/) walks through DIY test kits versus a professional air sample, and [signs of hidden mold](/blog/signs-of-hidden-mold/) covers what to look for before it's visible.

## Does Homeowners Insurance Cover Mold Remediation in Ventura?

Most homeowners policies only cover mold remediation when the mold traces back to a sudden, covered water loss, like a burst supply line, not from a long-term leak or humidity problem. Coverage is often capped at a set dollar amount even when it applies, so check your policy's mold endorsement before assuming it's covered in full.

Mold can begin growing within 24 to 48 hours on wet drywall or framing according to the [EPA](https://www.epa.gov/mold), which is why documenting the timeline of a water event matters for a claim. If your mold problem started with water damage, [our breakdown of mold growth timing after water damage](/blog/how-soon-does-mold-grow-after-water-damage/) explains that window in more detail, and the [Insurance Information Institute](https://www.iii.org/article/what-is-covered-by-standard-homeowners-insurance) has a general overview of what standard homeowners policies cover.

## What Happens During a Mold Remediation Job in Ventura?

A mold remediation job starts with a moisture inspection, moves into containment and source removal, and ends with air scrubbing and a clearance check before the area is released back to the homeowner. The crew isolates the affected area with poly sheeting and negative air pressure so spores don't spread to clean parts of the home during demolition.

Affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, sometimes subfloor, are removed and bagged for disposal. HEPA vacuuming and antimicrobial treatment follow, then air scrubbers run until particle counts drop to an acceptable level. A final visual and moisture check confirms the space is dry and ready for reconstruction.
