## Why Saticoy Properties See Water Damage Issues

Saticoy's low elevation near the Santa Clara River means groundwater sits closer to the surface than in surrounding Ventura County communities, and that affects how water moves once it gets inside a structure. Homes and outbuildings dating back to the area's citrus and agricultural era often have slab foundations poured decades before modern vapor barriers were standard, so a slow leak under a kitchen or laundry area can wick moisture sideways through the slab for weeks before a stain or odor gives it away. Add in irrigation lines that cross many Saticoy properties for remaining agricultural use, and a cracked supply line doesn't always show up where you'd expect it.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Saticoy

We start with moisture mapping, using meters to find out how far water has traveled beyond the visible wet spot, which matters more in Saticoy's older construction than it would in a newer subdivision. Extraction comes next, pulling standing water from flooring and, where needed, from crawl spaces that are common under the area's older single-story homes. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers placed based on the moisture map rather than a fixed formula, because a 1950s slab home dries on a different timeline than a stucco-and-drywall build from the last twenty years. We monitor daily until readings confirm the structure is dry, not just dry to the touch.
